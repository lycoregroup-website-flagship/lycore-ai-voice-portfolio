# Service-Business Websites and Lead-Capture Workflows

**Project type:** website design and implementation samples for U.S. service businesses.

## The client problem

A visitor needs to identify a service, trust the business, and contact someone fast. A site that looks polished but hides the phone number, makes the form difficult or routes enquiries nowhere isn't effective.

## Scope of the work

- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile, with service-specific calls to action.
- Home, service, service-area and contact-page structures for home-service businesses.
- Click-to-call, contact forms, appointment-request paths, and carefully worded trust elements.
- Website content audits for confusing navigation, weak calls to action, inaccurate business information and friction in enquiry flows.
- Lead-form and booking handoff design, including the information a CRM or notification workflow should receive.
- Readability, load-performance and basic accessibility checks.
- Website edits, maintenance and redesign recommendations based on the actual business and its audience.

## Representative project concepts

- **Towing and roadside assistance website designs:** urgent call-to-action hierarchy, emergency enquiries, location/service coverage and mobile usability.
- **Service-business lead-capture websites:** lead forms, callback requests, qualification fields and integration-ready submission flows.
- **Conversion-oriented website reviews:** site and Google Business Profile audits to identify friction before proposing changes.

These are design and project samples. This page does not claim measured conversion lifts, published client contracts or a live implementation where none is verified.

## Typical acceptance checklist

1. Important pages work on a small mobile screen.
2. Click-to-call and form submission controls work as expected.
3. Service area, service type, hours and next steps are easy to find.
4. Enquiry submissions go to the intended destination.
5. Consent fields and privacy disclosures are appropriate for the intended use.
6. Form failure, duplicate submissions and unsupported user input are handled.

**Portfolio materials to attach:** screenshots of approved website work or non-client prototypes, link to a publicly shareable sample that does not expose restricted contact details, and a brief before/after explanation of the changes made.
