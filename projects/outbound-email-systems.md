# B2B Outbound Prospecting, Cold Email and Deliverability Systems

**Project type:** outbound operations, lead-list quality and campaign infrastructure for U.S. B2B service-business prospecting.

## The workflow

1. **Define the ideal customer profile.** Choose business type, geography, role, service relevance and exclusion criteria.
2. **Source decision-makers.** Research business owners and contacts using tools such as Hunter.io and GetLeads.io; verify decision-maker relevance.
3. **Verify and clean lead data.** Validate email addresses, remove duplicates and exclude unsuitable or suppressed contacts.
4. **Configure sending infrastructure.** Separate outreach domains and mailboxes from the primary business domain; inspect authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), ramp-up, warm-up and sending volume.
5. **Build relevant sequences.** Write concise, specific prospecting messages and personalize around observable business needs, avoiding fabricated claims.
6. **Manage replies and follow-up.** Route interested contacts, honor opt-outs, track bounce/error signals and log outcomes.
7. **Review evidence.** Measure mailbox health, replies, positive responses and booked conversations rather than celebrating send volume alone.

## Quality and compliance

- Maintain suppression/opt-out handling and applicable anti-spam compliance.
- Do not send unsolicited messages to unverifiable or unrelated contacts.
- Verify both contact identity and email deliverability where appropriate.
- Protect prospect information and avoid uploading client lists or credentials to public repositories.
- Validate sending limits and infrastructure before increasing campaign volumes.
- Do not claim guaranteed inbox placement or invented reply/booked-meeting rates.
