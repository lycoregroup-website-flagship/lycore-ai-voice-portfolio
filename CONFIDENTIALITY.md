# Why these projects use made-up data

Real client work is confidential. An executive's calendar, inbox, travel plans, budgets and internal systems belong to that person and their company, so I do not post them, and a client would be right to expect that.

To show how I work without breaking that rule, every project in this repository is rebuilt from scratch on invented data:

- Companies and people are pseudonyms. Cedarline Partners, Northbank Group, Jordan Hale, Priya Nair and Tom Eze do not exist.
- Dates, amounts, emails and schedules are made up for the exercise. Email addresses use example.com.
- Screenshots are recreated illustrations built from the invented data, so no account, login or client system is ever visible.
- Nothing in these projects was sent, booked, paid or shared with anyone.

These are practice projects. They are not paid client work, and they are not a record of full-time executive assistant employment. They show the method, the judgement and the finished documents, which is what a client can check before hiring.

If you would like to see how I would handle your own situation, I am glad to do a short paid or unpaid trial task on your terms, under an agreement that keeps your information private.
